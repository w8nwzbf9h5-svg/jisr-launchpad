What the model assumes

  • One billion coins: 800 million at the fixed price of $0.0001, then today's curve on the last 200 million, without the stop at 190 million.
  • The basic fee is 5% of every buy and every sell: 2% to the reserve and 3% to the creator and the launchpad, as today.
  • A step counts once the market cap is 10% past it. Selling at the floor price always costs 5%; the brake applies only to what is paid above the floor.
  • The top is reached by buying with no selling, then everyone sells with no buying. A real market moves both ways.
  • This is arithmetic in your browser, not a contract. The figures are approximate, and new contracts would need their own tests and an audit before any real money.
