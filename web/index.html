Launch a coin whose floor is paid for in advance.

Jisr sells every coin at one fixed price and keeps the money in a reserve that no person controls. Any holder can sell back to that reserve at the floor price, at any time. Move the handle on the chart and watch the reserve keep up.

There is no cheap early round and the creator receives no coins. A wallet may hold 2.5% of a coin at most. The whole method is in the white paper, in ten languages.

    How a coin lives

    The same four stages for every coin, on every network.

    1. LaunchThe creator pays the network fee and receives no coins. All one billion go to the coin's own market.
    2. Sale800 million coins at one fixed price. The value of every coin sold goes into the reserve.
    3. GraduationThe sale sells out. The reserve holds about $81,684 for those 800 million coins.
    4. After graduationThe last 200 million sell above the floor, and fees from trading keep raising the floor.

    What exists today

    A plain account, including what is not done.

    Networks
    Solana devnet and Ethereum Sepolia. Both are test networks.
    Solana program
    Deployed with its upgrade authority removed, so nobody can change it. The check above reads that from the network.
    Ethereum contracts
    47 automated contract tests and 25 browser test steps. Markets have no owner, no pause and no withdrawal.
    Fees
    5% of each trade: 1% to the coin's creator, 2% to the launchpad, 2% into the coin's own reserve. Paid out automatically as the coin trades.
    Independent audit
    Not done yet.
    Real money
    Not yet. Nothing is deployed on a real network.
