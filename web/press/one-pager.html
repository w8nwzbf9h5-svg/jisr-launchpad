LANDINGPAD

A meme coin launchpad where the floor is paid for in advance.

Every coin sold puts its full price into a reserve that no person controls. Any holder can sell back to that reserve at the floor price, at any time. The floor rises with trading and is never lowered.

The problem

Most meme coins crash within days. Early wallets and creator bags sell into a pool that only exists while people keep buying. Late buyers are left with coins nobody will buy back.

How Landing Pad works

  • 1B supply per coin; no presale, no team allocation.
  • First 800M coins at one fixed price, full value into the coin's reserve.
  • Last 200M on a curve above the floor.
  • Sell back to the reserve at the floor, any time.

What it rules out

  • Creator bags: the creator gets zero coins and earns 1% of trading fees.
  • Pulled liquidity: the reserve has no withdraw.
  • Whales: one wallet holds at most 2.5%.

Tested in public

A stress test in the browser runs 60 traders, or one attacker with 40 wallets, and checks after every trade that the reserve can still buy back every coin at the floor. All code and tests are public on GitHub.

Status

Public test on Solana devnet and Ethereum Sepolia, with test money. Contracts not audited yet. Mainnet opens at 100 pieces of tester feedback, or when $LPAD reaches a $1M market cap.
